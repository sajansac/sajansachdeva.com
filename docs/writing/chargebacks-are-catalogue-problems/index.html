Chargebacks Are Catalogue Problems

400 penalties look like 400 problems. They're usually 12.

Most vendors treat chargebacks as individual incidents. A late shipment, an ASN mismatch, a shortage dispute — each one gets filed or eaten.

But the pattern is almost always structural. When you map six months of chargeback data for a single business, the same 15–20 product codes keep surfacing. Not because they’re unlucky — because something in the catalogue setup or operational workflow was never configured properly.

A prep requirement that nobody set. A packaging certification that was never completed. A label format that mismatches the receiving system. Each one generates a per-unit penalty every single shipment, month after month.

The interesting thing is that AI’s value here isn’t speed — it’s pattern recognition across volume. A human looking at 400 individual chargebacks sees 400 problems. A system that clusters them sees 12 root causes. Fix those 12, and 70% of the total disappears.

The unsexy problems are always the most expensive ones.
