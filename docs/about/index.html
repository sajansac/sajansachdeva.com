About

I’m an operator and product manager based in London. For nearly ten years I’ve worked on the unglamorous machinery of e-commerce: how products get bought, shipped, received and paid for. First at Flipkart in Bangalore, then at Amazon in Bangalore and London.

At Amazon I’m a Senior Product Manager in Vendor Experience, where I work on sourcing lead times, delivery windows and vendor chargebacks across Europe. In January 2026 I co-founded FlowPotato, an AI operations co-pilot for product brands.

I write about the space between business and AI: where it earns its keep, where it doesn’t, and what it takes to turn operational judgment into software.
